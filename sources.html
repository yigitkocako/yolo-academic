Hello! I’m Yiğit Koçak.

Like many of you, after completing secondary school and university in Türkiye, I wanted to pursue postgraduate studies abroad.

I moved to Dublin, Ireland, where I completed my master’s studies. I went on to experience job hunting, career development and building a life here first-hand. I have lived in Dublin for around seven years, working on YouTube and Meta projects at Accenture.

Drawing on my experience at CDS Consultancy in Türkiye and my own education in Ireland, I founded YOLO Academic to offer professional guidance and share what I have learned.

At YOLO Academic we believe choosing the right programme and career direction matters. For those who need it, we can first arrange guidance with a professional career adviser in our team to clarify interests and abilities, before meeting online to explore appropriate applications in Ireland.

Together we consider language schools, secondary education, undergraduate and postgraduate routes in Ireland, including budgets, application dates, visa preparation, documents, accommodation research and settling in after arrival.

Studying abroad is about more than picking a school: it means choosing a sustainable future built on the right country, programme and budget. We want to help plan that together.

If you want to study in Ireland, develop your career or make a fresh start abroad, you can contact me through YOLO Academic. YOLO stands for You Only Live Once.

With the right preparation, the journey begins on stronger ground. I’m here when you need me.
